# Code of Conduct

FreeMyDisk is built by people from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want every one of them to feel welcome here — whether they're opening their first issue or reviewing their hundredth pull request.

## Our standards

**We do:**

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- give feedback on the work, not the person — and make it specific and constructive;
- accept feedback gracefully, and credit other people's ideas and work;
- focus on what's best for the people who use FreeMyDisk.

**We don't:**

- harass, insult, demean or threaten anyone, in public or in private;
- make comments about anyone's identity, appearance, background or skill level;
- use sexualised language or imagery;
- publish anyone's private information without their permission;
- keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop;
- behave in any way that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [@fzihak](https://github.com/fzihak), through GitHub (or, for anything sensitive, via the private *Report a vulnerability* form under the Security tab). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in proportion to the behaviour: from a private note, to a public warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may be removed.
